# Advisory AI assistance

The user requested less authoritative decision review, explicit evidence gaps, preservation of minority views, optional small hints and meaning-preserving rewrites.

The engine retains the stored `reviewer_pass` rule name and `pass` score field for compatibility. Their interpretation changes: readiness is advice and never automatically advances a decision. The solo participant or facilitator accepts the resolved decision, with a reason required when advice is absent, uncertain, unverified or identifies concerns. Acceptance stores the proposal, voting round, actor, timestamp and reason. Decision timeouts cannot substitute for acceptance. Old reviews remain historical when votes restart.

Current method rubrics contain criterion-specific scoring anchors. Reviewer output requires concern categories and reasons. Quotes are checked against actual proposal/reveal material, now including original responses, rather than rubric illustrations. Quote matching is explicitly described as a limited safeguard.

Clustering preserves source access and deterministically restores omitted responses as additional perspectives. The prompt retains outliers, uncertainty and unresolved disagreement and forbids equating theme membership with consensus. This does not guarantee perfect interpretation; originals remain available for human correction.

Help is participant-initiated and optional. Examples and pass/swap options can be reached without asking for an AI hint. Rewrites are compared with the original; choosing a suggestion changes only the editor until the facilitator publishes it. Stale suggestions cannot silently overwrite changed wording.

The optional solo “What have I missed?” checkpoint remains deferred. No additional provider or recurring service was introduced.
